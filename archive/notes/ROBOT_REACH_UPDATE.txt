SMARTGARAGE - ROBOT FULL VEHICLE COVERAGE UPDATE
================================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. Robot reach
   - Shoulder/lower link: 4.50 scene units
   - Forearm/upper link: 4.00 scene units
   - The arm can now span across the central Cybertruck to its right side and
     reach front/rear corners while the vehicle is on the lift.

2. Head movement
   - Y = tilt head one direction
   - H = tilt head the opposite direction
   - Head pitch is clamped to -90..+90 degrees: exactly 180 degrees total.
   - LEFT/RIGHT still controls tool roll independently.

3. Collision behaviour
   - The heavy shoulder link remains blocked by the Cybertruck.
   - During manual arm positioning, forearm + wrist/tool may enter the vehicle's
     service envelope so the tool can reach the opposite/right side.
   - During lift movement, the whole robot/tool becomes a hard obstacle again.
   - Lift mechanism, pedestal, tool station, floor, ceiling and auxiliary lifts
     remain protected by collision checks.

ROBOT CONTROLS
--------------
T / G          base yaw
U / J          shoulder
I / K          elbow
Y / H          180-degree top/bottom head tilt
LEFT / RIGHT   wrist/tool roll
F1..F4         scanner / driver / gripper / polisher
