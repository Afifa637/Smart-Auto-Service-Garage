SMARTGARAGE AUTO SERVICE V4 - FINAL ALIGNMENT FIX

What changed
------------
1. Surface tool orientation corrected mathematically.
   The scanner, cleaning sponge and polisher now face the Cybertruck side panel
   instead of pointing downward or letting the wrist/head pass through the body.

2. Surface poses calibrated to the actual rendered service patch.
   Target panel is the left side of the service Cybertruck around x=-1.10 with
   the sweep following the same z-range used by the scan/dirt/polish effects.

3. Dedicated sponge poses are now actually used.
   The sponge extends farther than the scanner/polisher, so its six cleaning
   poses are separately retracted to place the foam on the body surface while
   keeping the gripper outside.

4. Impact-driver positions recalibrated to the five rendered lug nuts.
   The socket axis faces the wheel hub.  Each target is solved against the
   actual front-left lug location, then hard-coded as a simple predefined pose.

5. Wrist pitch range expanded to +/-270 degrees.
   This is only angle wrapping for the wrist orientation.  There is still no
   runtime inverse kinematics, physics, path planning or AI.

How to run
----------
Open YoutubeOpenGL.slnx in Visual Studio.
Build x64 / Rebuild Solution.
Run with Ctrl+F5.
Window title must contain:
  SmartGarage AUTO SERVICE v4 - Exact Surface Alignment
Press ENTER to start. Press R to reset.
